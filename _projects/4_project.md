As a **BioSoc project mentor at IIT Kanpur (Apr–Dec 2024)**, I delivered seven lectures and tutorials on computational biology and bioinformatics, and led students applying machine learning to biological datasets.

The Biobytes teaching repository, co-led with **Sikha Vamsi**, contains curriculum notebooks, assignments, and case studies on DNA sequences and drug discovery. Topics include missing-data handling, cross-validation, feature engineering, clustering, and bioactivity prediction using ChEMBL data and molecular descriptors.

Related computational project work explored ChEMBL-based modeling of Influenza A bioactivity using SMILES, Lipinski and PaDEL descriptors, Random Forest, and KNN.

The repository includes **reference tutorials and mentee submissions**, rather than exclusively original research code. Its acetylcholinesterase drug-discovery notebooks follow Chanin Nantasenamat's (Data Professor) tutorial series; mentee work applies the pipeline to a coronavirus target.
