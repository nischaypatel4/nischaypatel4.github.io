## Research context

**TRANSIT Lab · IIT Kanpur · Jun–Dec 2025**  
Supervised by **Prof. Pragathi Balasubramani**.

This research integrated audio, video, and transcript features to study mental-health outcomes and depression subtypes.

## Approach

The pipeline combined summary statistics and temporal representations with **Whisper** speech transcripts and **BERT** embeddings. Feature engineering included mutual-information selection, PCA, and SMOTE; SHAP, sentiment analysis, radar plots, and correlations supported interpretation of modality-specific signals.

The accompanying public repository describes multimodal analysis of the E-DAIC dataset. These are research models; the repository alone does not establish clinical validation or deployment readiness.

## Publication and recognition

I am a co-author of the 2026 _BioSystems_ paper, **“Model development and feasibility of real-world deployment of multimodal input-based subtyping of depression in tele-counseling for scalable mental health assessment.”**

Research in depression prediction and subtyping received the **Ranjan Kumar Memorial Award** for the best socially relevant project at IIT Kanpur's 59th Convocation.
