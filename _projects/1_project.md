During the **SRIP research internship at IIT Gandhinagar (May–Jul 2024)** with **Prof. Subramanian Sankaranarayanan**, I worked on distinguishing inter- and intra-species variants of Bougainvillea, Plumeria, and Dianthus using DNA barcoding-based molecular profiling.

The work led to a first-author publication in the _Journal of Applied Biology and Biotechnology_.
