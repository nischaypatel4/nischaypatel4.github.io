## Research context

**BSE662 course project · IIT Kanpur · Jan–Apr 2025**  
Supervised by **Prof. Arjun Ramakrishnan**.

This project investigated relationships between trait anxiety and decisions under uncertainty, using EEG and behavioral data from a sequential foraging task.

## Approach

I worked with EEG preprocessing and feature extraction, including band power, entropy, event-related potentials, phase-amplitude coupling, and connectivity features. These representations were studied alongside behavioral measures and cortisol/salivary alpha-amylase markers.

Classical machine-learning and neural-network models explored trait-anxiety prediction and stay/leave decisions. The public repository contains analysis notebooks and reports. The work is a **course-based computational study**, rather than a clinical diagnostic tool.
