## Research context

At the **Lab of Neural Systems, IIT Kanpur (Aug 2024–Mar 2025)**, I worked on an undergraduate project under the supervision of **Prof. Nitin Gupta**.

The project involved designing primers and performing PCR to identify off-target CRISPR-Cas9 insertions in a transgenic _Aedes aegypti_ line with sex-ratio distortion. I also analyzed transcription-factor binding sites, focusing on the doublesex (_dsx_) protein and its role in sexual differentiation.

## Methods

Primer design, PCR, analysis of CRISPR-Cas9 off-target regions, and transcription-factor binding-site analysis using JASPAR. Protein–DNA docking and sequence-specific binding analysis were used to investigate potential effects on doublesex isoforms.
