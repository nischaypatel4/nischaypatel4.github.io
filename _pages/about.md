I am a computational biology researcher interested in developing statistical, machine learning, and generative approaches to understanding complex biological systems, particularly in genomics. My research interests span single-cell and spatial transcriptomics, representation learning, and gene-regulatory network inference, with a broader focus on genome regulation and disease mechanisms.

I have worked with the PMDCB Lab at IIT Gandhinagar and the Lab of Neural Systems at IIT Kanpur, and was an Amgen Scholar at Tsinghua University, where I explored representation-based approaches to cross-tissue analysis of single-cell data.

Beyond research, I enjoy playing tennis and cricket, mastering touch typing, and writing blogs about my experiences.
